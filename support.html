Common questions

The Add pages button is disabled. Your design's size doesn't match the book size you picked. Create a custom size design at that size, or choose the matching book size.

Adding pages pauses. Canva limits how quickly apps can add pages. The app waits and continues automatically; a 120-page book takes about two minutes.

Which margins are used? Every page gets the same margin on both sides: the larger of Amazon's inside margin for your page count and a 0.5 in outside margin. The top margin is 0.6 in and the bottom margin is 0.5 in.

Does this guarantee Amazon approval? No. It helps with page size and margins, but Amazon reviews each book against its own content guidelines.
