Notebook Maker

Privacy policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Notebook Maker ("the app") is a Canva app made by Usama Mumtaz.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app runs entirely inside Canva in your browser. It has no server, no account, no analytics, and no advertising. It does not send your designs or personal information anywhere.

What the app accesses

With your permission in Canva, the app reads and changes the design you have open, only to add pages and to check or move elements for the margin check. This happens in your browser and is not stored by the app.

Canva

Your use of Canva is covered by Canva's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
